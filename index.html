Yun Deng

I am a computational population geneticist interested in developing statistical and computational methods for understanding genome evolution and population history.

I am currently a postdoctoral scholar in the Department of Genetics at Stanford University, working with Jonathan Pritchard. I received my Ph.D. in Computational Biology from the University of California, Berkeley, where I worked with Rasmus Nielsen and Yun S. Song.

Research

My research focuses on population genetics, statistical genomics, and ancestral recombination graphs (ARGs). I develop computational methods for reconstructing genome-wide genealogies and using them to study population history, natural selection, genetic variation, and other evolutionary processes.
